# Public real Change expansion selection

`efforthours-change-public-real-expansion/0.1.0` freezes six immutable public
pull-request cases before any candidate report is generated or any numerical
teacher review begins. All six repositories expose an MIT license whose exact blob
is unchanged between the selected base and head.

The matrix adds six new repository families: two .NET, two JavaScript, and two
TypeScript. Three families are development, two are validation, and one is test.
The cases cover a public selection feature, a rendering termination defect,
concurrency receiver behavior, browser-protocol cancellation, security-sensitive
parser output, and request-hook normalization across small, medium, and integration
scopes.

Selection used public PR titles, specifications, immutable object IDs, affected
surface names, and license provenance. Candidate hours, confidence, contributor
identity, activity, elapsed time, actual labor, commit count, and intermediate
churn were not selection inputs and cannot become effort multipliers.

The released alpha.2 reports will be written without displaying their numerical
content. Teacher review will use blind authoring packets plus the final public
specification, immutable final delta, and bounded adjacent source. The teacher plan
must be complete before candidate reports are opened. Development and validation
may then be evaluated diagnostically; the test comparison remains withheld.
