# EHE work-item review rubric 1.1.0

## Purpose

Produce consistent weak-supervision labels for EffortHours's Equivalent Human Effort
model. A label estimates the counterfactual effort for one competent senior
contractor, unfamiliar with the business domain and not using AI, to recreate the
current represented behavior and quality from a clear specification with sensible
2026 technology.

The result is a reviewed logical estimate. It is not actual labor, a timesheet, an
invoice, or literal ground truth.

Version 1.1.0 adds an explicit representation for reviewed false-positive
exclusions. Positive-label semantics are unchanged from version 1.0.0.

## Inputs

Start with the canonical estimate or compact work-item view and its evidence
lineage. Use `eh explain` for uncertain or material capabilities. Inspect
selected source only when the compressed evidence cannot resolve a consequential
question.

Do not inspect or use commit counts, churn, authors, timestamps, review duration,
or abandoned revisions as effort evidence. Do not use replacement cost or hourly
rates while labeling effort.

## Review procedure

1. Confirm the repository source digest, estimation profile, worker baseline, and
   static/verified working-state assumptions.
2. Check that represented capabilities correspond to meaningful current behavior,
   tests, documentation, integrations, delivery artifacts, and quality.
3. Exclude duplicate, dead, conventional generated, vendored, minified, and
   accidental implementation volume. Retain meaningful customization and
   integration work when evidence supports it.
4. Regroup source work-item parts when needed so each reviewed target represents
   one coherent task. Assign every source work-item ID at most once.
5. For represented work, keep target expected effort normally between 0.5 and 8
   hours. Supply a concrete size-exception reason when an indivisible positive item
   falls outside that range.
6. When a source target is an explicit false positive or entirely excluded under
   this rubric, use exactly `0/0/0` low, expected, and high hours. Record both a
   concrete rationale and `sizeException` explaining the exclusion. Do not use a
   partially positive zero range, and never use zero merely to express uncertainty,
   reuse, lower value, or a discount.
7. Estimate low, expected, and high EHE for each represented target. Low represents
   an unusually smooth competent implementation; expected represents the most
   defensible planning value; high represents plausible ambiguity and
   implementation risk.
8. Record a short rationale tied to evidence and list unresolved uncertainty. Do
   not widen ranges merely because the repository was not executed; use the actual
   verification warning and relevant technical ambiguity.
9. Check category totals and the repository total for coherence only after the
   small targets are labeled. Do not force labels to a preferred overall number.
10. Keep professionalization/remediation gaps outside represented targets.

The low/high values are review bounds, not formal probability quantiles until
later calibration demonstrates that interpretation. An exact-zero exclusion is a
classification decision, not an uncertainty interval.

## Profiles

For `implementation`, assume detailed requirements, acceptance criteria, and
available product/design inputs. For `recreation`, add only the explicit work needed
to recover or make architecture, data, interface, and UX decisions embodied in the
artifact. Never apply an opaque repository-wide profile multiplier.

## Review maturity

- `teacher-estimate`: one consistent teacher produced the labels.
- `reviewed`: another qualified reviewer checked and corrected material labels.
- `adjudicated`: disagreements were explicitly resolved.

AI estimates are weak supervision even when they are strong and internally
consistent. Record the available model identity and version. Human reviewers use a
stable publishable identifier; personal data is not required.

## Publication and privacy

Every record must declare source, revision/snapshot, license, data classification,
and redistribution permission. Do not place private client evidence, source,
credentials, names, or proprietary rationales in the public corpus. A private
record may use the public schema and evaluator locally without becoming a EffortHours
project asset.
