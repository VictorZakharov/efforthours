# Change EHE work-item review rubric 1.1.0

## Purpose

Produce reproducible logical labels for the Equivalent Human Effort represented by
one immutable final software change. A label estimates the time one competent
senior contractor, unfamiliar with the business domain and not using AI, would need
to recreate the final functional and quality delta from a clear specification with
sensible 2026 technology.

The label is a counterfactual engineering estimate. It is not actual labor,
elapsed time, a timesheet, an invoice, contributor productivity, or literal ground
truth.

## Label authority and limits

A disclosed human or host-AI teacher may author a logical calibration label. A
host-AI label records the available model identity, the input boundary, the
immutable source provenance, and the evidence-backed rationale. It must never be
relabeled as human or independently reviewed.

Logical validity comes from exact, inspectable decomposition: the category and
change totals are the sums of smaller tasks whose evidence and reasoning stand on
their own. A model-authored label is therefore useful weak supervision for an
experimental estimator. It does not establish empirical accuracy. Later blinded
production observations compare predictions with real outcomes under a separately
documented observation policy.

## Required inputs

Use a canonical `ChangeEstimateReport` and its matching Change calibration packet.
Confirm before assigning hours:

- stable repository-family and case identities;
- immutable base and head object identities;
- base and head evidence digests plus the derived final-delta digest;
- estimation profile and worker baseline;
- source, revision pair, license, redistribution, and partition provenance;
- the report's normalized final evidence, exclusions, reconciliation,
  diagnostics, and verification state; and
- for host-AI labels, the available model/version and the exact supplied context.

Use `eh change explain` for uncertain or material work items. Inspect bounded
base/head source only when compressed evidence cannot resolve a consequential
question. Do not require source excerpts in the public corpus.

## Forbidden signals

Never use commit count, intermediate churn, message length, author identity,
timestamps, branch shape, review discussion, elapsed duration, logged hours,
price, or a preferred total as effort evidence. Component estimates may explain
additivity, overlap, or reverts, but the normalized final base-to-head delta is
authoritative.

## Review procedure

1. Verify that each represented path corresponds to meaningful final behavior,
   tests, documentation, configuration, migration, integration, delivery, or
   simplification work.
2. Exclude formatting-only changes, exact mechanical movement or copying,
   conventional generated output, vendored/minified content, lockfiles, build
   output, and accidental volume. Preserve supported customization, generator
   integration, and validation work.
3. Review the final delta as a competent clean implementation. Do not reward
   duplication, poor decomposition, or repeated historical attempts.
4. Treat deletion as bounded positive design, implementation, migration, and
   validation work when it deliberately changes the final system. Never price
   deleted volume and never make deletion negative effort.
5. Decompose from evidence upward. Aim for targets of roughly 0.5 to 1.5 expected
   hours. A target above 2 expected hours requires a concrete explanation of why
   further logical separation would be artificial; the existing 8-hour contract
   ceiling remains a hard upper bound.
6. Give each target a distinct task statement, evidence lineage, expected result,
   rationale, and uncertainty. Ten expected hours should ordinarily appear as
   approximately ten independently defensible one-hour tasks, not one ten-hour
   assertion or ten copies of the same explanation.
7. Regroup source work-item parts only for coherent tasks. Assign each source
   work-item ID at most once and preserve its evidence IDs. Reject a false positive,
   duplicate, or excluded mechanical concern with exact `0/0/0` hours plus a
   concrete rationale and `sizeException` so its lineage remains visible.
8. A normalized final delta with no represented work uses an empty target list and
   an exact zero total. Do not invent a zero-hour task for formatting, movement,
   conventional generation, or a complete revert.
9. Estimate low, expected, and high EHE for every represented target. Low is an
   unusually smooth competent implementation, expected is the most defensible
   planning value, and high covers plausible ambiguity and implementation risk.
10. Sum targets exactly into categories and the final change total only after all
    target estimates are frozen. For disjoint no-rework cases, separately verify
    the documented additivity tolerance; never alter targets to reach a desired
    total.

## Progressive size ladder

Calibration begins with changes whose expected EHE is roughly one to several
senior-contractor days. Exact-zero and sub-day semantic guardrails remain useful,
but they do not by themselves advance the size ladder.

Advance only after the current band meets its frozen logical-agreement and safety
gates:

1. one-to-several-day changes, normally about 4 to 32 expected hours;
2. multi-day to multi-week changes; and
3. larger deliverables only after the preceding band remains stable.

Record host-AI input size, model identity, wall time, token use, and cost when the
client can provide them. Review expense is a measurement, never an effort
multiplier. Increasing source context or model cost does not justify skipping the
smaller-size gate.

## Profiles

For `implementation`, assume detailed requirements and available product/design
inputs. For `recreation`, add only explicit work needed to recover or make design
decisions embodied in the final delta. Never apply an opaque change-wide profile
multiplier.

## Coverage tags

Coverage tags select corpus strata and do not affect estimated hours. Tags are
lowercase kebab-case and identify material semantics such as `clean-disjoint`,
`overlap`, `revert`, `deletion`, `tests`, `documentation`, `integration`,
`delivery`, `generated-conventional`, or `generated-customization`, plus the
relevant ecosystem and size band.

## Review maturity

- `teacher-estimate`: one disclosed human or host-AI teacher supplied the logical
  labels. Under this rubric, a fully decomposed host-AI teacher estimate may be
  admitted as logical weak supervision for an experimental model.
- `reviewed`: a genuinely distinct reviewer blindly checked every target and
  deliberately accepted or replaced it.
- `adjudicated`: disagreements were explicitly resolved by an adjudicator.

Independent review remains useful corroboration but is not required for logical
teacher-label admission. Generating a blind packet, copying candidate values, or
mechanically compiling a plan does not change its recorded maturity.

## Publication and privacy

Every record declares source, exact base/head revisions, license, data
classification, redistribution permission, reviewer provenance, and repository
family. Keep all changes from one repository family in one partition. Never commit
private client evidence, credentials, personal data, proprietary source, or
unlicensed examples to the public corpus.
