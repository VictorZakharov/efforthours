# Change EHE work-item review rubric 1.0.0

## Purpose

Produce consistent weak-supervision labels for the Equivalent Human Effort
represented by one immutable final software change. A label estimates the time one
competent senior contractor, unfamiliar with the business domain and not using AI,
would need to recreate the final functional and quality delta from a clear
specification with sensible 2026 technology.

The result is a reviewed logical estimate. It is not actual labor, elapsed time, a
timesheet, an invoice, contributor productivity, or literal ground truth.

## Required inputs

Use a canonical `ChangeEstimateReport` and its matching Change calibration packet.
Confirm the following before assigning hours:

- stable repository-family and case identities;
- immutable base and head object identities;
- base and head evidence digests plus the derived final-delta digest;
- estimation profile and worker baseline;
- source, revision pair, license, redistribution, and partition provenance; and
- the report's normalized final evidence, work items, exclusions, reconciliation,
  diagnostics, and verification state.

Use `fairbill change explain` for uncertain or material work items. Inspect bounded
base/head source only when compressed evidence cannot resolve a consequential
question. Do not require source excerpts in the public corpus.

## Forbidden signals

Never use commit count, intermediate churn, message length, author identity,
timestamps, branch shape, review discussion, elapsed duration, price, or a
preferred total as effort evidence. Component estimates may explain additivity,
overlap, or reverts, but the normalized final base-to-head delta is authoritative.

## Review procedure

1. Verify that each represented path corresponds to meaningful final behavior,
   tests, documentation, configuration, migration, integration, delivery, or
   simplification work.
2. Exclude formatting-only changes, exact mechanical movement or copying,
   conventional generated output, vendored/minified content, lockfiles, build
   output, and accidental volume. Preserve supported customization, generator
   integration, and validation work.
3. Review the final delta as a competent clean implementation. Do not reward
   duplication, poor decomposition, or repeated historical attempts.
4. Treat deletion as bounded positive design, implementation, migration, and
   validation work when it deliberately changes the final system. Never price
   deleted volume and never make deletion negative effort.
5. Regroup source work-item parts only when needed for a coherent task. Assign each
   source work-item ID at most once and preserve its evidence IDs. When a source
   capability is a false positive, duplicate, or excluded mechanical concern, use
   an exact `0/0/0` target plus a concrete rationale and `sizeException`. This keeps
   its source lineage visible to evaluation and independent review.
6. Keep represented target expected effort normally between 0.5 and 8 hours.
   Supply a concrete size exception for an indivisible target outside that range.
7. A normalized final delta with no represented work uses an empty target list and
   therefore an exact zero total. Do not invent a zero-hour work item merely to
   record formatting, movement, conventional generation, or a complete revert.
8. Estimate low, expected, and high EHE for every represented target. Low is an
   unusually smooth competent implementation, expected is the most defensible
   planning value, and high covers plausible ambiguity and implementation risk.
9. Record a concise evidence-backed rationale and concrete uncertainty reasons.
   Keep professionalization/remediation gaps outside represented targets.
10. Reconcile category and case totals only after target review. For disjoint
    no-rework cases, separately verify the documented additivity tolerance; do not
    force individual targets to make a range total desirable.

## Profiles

For `implementation`, assume detailed requirements and available product/design
inputs. For `recreation`, add only explicit work needed to recover or make design
decisions embodied in the final delta. Never apply an opaque change-wide profile
multiplier.

## Coverage tags

Coverage tags select corpus strata and do not affect estimated hours. Tags are
lowercase kebab-case and should identify the material semantics under review, such
as `clean-disjoint`, `overlap`, `revert`, `deletion`, `tests`, `documentation`,
`integration`, `delivery`, `generated-conventional`, or
`generated-customization`, plus the relevant ecosystem.

## Review maturity

- `teacher-estimate`: one consistent human or host-AI teacher supplied labels.
- `reviewed`: a genuinely distinct reviewer blindly checked every target and
  deliberately accepted or replaced it.
- `adjudicated`: disagreements were explicitly resolved by an adjudicator.

Generating a blind packet, copying candidate values, or mechanically compiling a
plan does not advance maturity. Host-AI reviewers record the available model and
version. An independent reviewer must not be the agent that created the first-pass
labels.

## Publication and privacy

Every record declares source, exact base/head revisions, license, data
classification, redistribution permission, reviewer provenance, and repository
family. Keep all changes from one repository family in one partition. Never commit
private client evidence, credentials, personal data, proprietary source, or
unlicensed examples to the public corpus.
