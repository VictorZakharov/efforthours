# Fairbill Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Fairbill welcomes people who disagree constructively about software, estimation,
compensation, and the meaning of engineering work. Participants must remain
respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.

Expected behavior includes:

- treating contributors and users with patience and professional respect;
- criticizing ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- acknowledging uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and accidental mistakes;
- protecting private source, credentials, and identifying client information; and
- accepting reasonable maintainer direction about scope and community safety.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private
communications, or repeatedly disrupting productive discussion.

## Scope

This standard applies in the Fairbill repository, issue tracker, pull requests,
reviews, release discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Contact the repository
owner through a private channel listed on their GitHub profile. If a report also
describes a security vulnerability, follow `SECURITY.md`.

The maintainer will review reports in good faith, protect the reporter's privacy as
far as practical, and may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, limit
participation, or permanently exclude a participant. Enforcement decisions should
be proportionate, documented privately, and free from retaliation.
